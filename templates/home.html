User Flows

Two guided journeys built around the same NLP engine.

TalentLens is structured as a dual-portal platform. Each side has its own workspace, but both rely on the same explainable resume-analysis pipeline.

Job Seeker Flow

Upload or paste, analyze, improve.

01

Choose your input mode and submit resume content.

02

Get role prediction, confidence, completeness, and evidence.

03

Use missing skills and improvement tips to strengthen the profile.

Recruiter Flow

Add candidates, rank them, compare the strongest.

01

Define the role and add candidate resumes by upload or paste.

02

Generate ranked results using similarity, fit, and coverage signals.

03

Compare top candidates side by side before final shortlisting.
